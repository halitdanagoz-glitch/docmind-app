Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DocMind handles your data when you use the app. We are committed to transparency about what we collect, where it goes, and why.

Developer: Halit Danagöz Bundle ID: com.halit.docmind Contact: halit@danagoz.com

What This App Does

DocMind is an AI-powered document assistant. You can upload or scan documents, and the app uses artificial intelligence to help you understand, summarize, and interact with the content.

Data Sent to Third-Party AI Services

To provide AI-powered analysis, DocMind sends document content to Anthropic, Inc. via their Claude API. This is a third-party AI service.

What is sent

The text content of documents you upload or scan within the app.

Who receives it

Anthropic, Inc. — the provider of the Claude AI model used for document analysis.

Why it is sent

To perform AI-based analysis, summarization, and question-answering on your documents.

User consent

The app asks for your explicit permission before sending any document content to Anthropic's API. You may decline, in which case AI features will not be available.

Anthropic's privacy policy is available at: anthropic.com/privacy

Data We Collect

  • Document content (text extracted from uploaded or scanned files) — processed via Anthropic API, not stored by us
  • App usage data — used only for crash reporting and app stability (no personal identification)

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Data Storage

DocMind does not store your document content on any server we operate. Documents are processed locally on your device or temporarily transmitted to Anthropic for AI analysis and are not retained by us after processing.

Anthropic's data retention practices are governed by their own privacy policy linked above.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party other than Anthropic as described above. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling purposes.

Children's Privacy

DocMind is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

  • You can stop using the app at any time and delete it from your device
  • You can decline AI processing consent — the app will not send your documents to Anthropic
  • For any privacy-related requests, contact us at halit@danagoz.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
