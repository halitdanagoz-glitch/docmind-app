DocMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

DocMind processes document images and text locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework. When you use AI features, the extracted text from your documents is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. We do not store your document content on our servers after processing is complete.

2. How We Use Your Information

Document text is used solely to provide AI analysis features including summaries, categorization, and question answering. We do not sell, share, or use your document data for advertising or any purpose other than providing the DocMind service.

3. Third-Party Services

DocMind uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API: Document text is sent to Anthropic for AI analysis. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription transactions. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Hosts API functions that relay requests to Anthropic. Data is not stored.

4. Data Storage

Your documents, PDFs, and extracted text are stored locally on your device only. You can delete all data by deleting the app.

5. User Consent

Upon first launch, users must explicitly consent to document text being sent to Anthropic for AI analysis before using AI features.

6. Subscriptions

Subscription management is handled through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

7. Children's Privacy

DocMind is not directed at children under 13.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@docmind.app
